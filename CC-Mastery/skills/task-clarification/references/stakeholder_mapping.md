# Stakeholder Mapping Template

Use this template when running `/stakeholders` to identify all parties with requirements influence.

## Stakeholder Categories

### Customer (Decision Maker)
- Who approves the purchase?
- What is the budget?
- What is the required timeline?
- What are the contractual obligations?

### User (Operator)
- Who operates the system daily?
- What is their technical skill level?
- What are the operating conditions (field, lab, office)?
- What training is acceptable?

### Maintainer (Support)
- Who performs maintenance and repair?
- What tools and skills are available?
- What is the acceptable maintenance frequency?
- Field-level vs depot-level capability?

### Regulator (Compliance)
- Which standards apply (MIL-STD, TCVN)?
- What is the approval/certification process?
- What documentation is required?
- Export control considerations?

### Manufacturer (Production)
- What production capabilities exist locally?
- What technologies are available in Vietnam?
- What is the local content requirement (60-75%)?
- Supply chain constraints?

## Stakeholder Influence Matrix

| Stakeholder | Influence | Interest | Strategy |
|-------------|-----------|----------|----------|
| Customer | High/Med/Low | High/Med/Low | Manage closely / Keep satisfied / Keep informed / Monitor |

## Output

For each stakeholder, document:
1. Name and role
2. Key requirements they drive
3. Conflicts with other stakeholders
4. Sign-off needed (yes/no)
