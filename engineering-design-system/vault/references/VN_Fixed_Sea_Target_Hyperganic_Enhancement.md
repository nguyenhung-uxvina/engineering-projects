### 2.2 Không phải MỌI THỨ cần Hyperganic

**Musk principle: "Use the right tool for the right job"**

| Component | Traditional Fab | Hyperganic/AM | **Decision** | Lý do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Superstructure frame | Steel welding | Topology opt. joints | **HYBRID** — Frame traditional, joints AM | Cost-effective |
| Corner reflectors | Sheet metal bending | AM printing | **AM** ✅ | Precision 10x better, integrated mount |
| Flotation pontoons | HDPE hollow | TPMS multi-compartment | **AM** ✅✅ | Game-changer — unsinkable |
| Mooring hardware | Standard marine | N/A | **TRADITIONAL** | No benefit from AM |
| IR heater panels | Resistive elements | Schwarz P channels | **AM** ✅ | Realistic thermal distribution |
| GPS/Instrumentation | COTS electronics | N/A | **TRADITIONAL** | Electronics ≠ AM application |
| Deck plating | Steel plate | N/A | **TRADITIONAL** | Large flat surfaces = waste of AM |

**Kết luận: ~30% of target by mass benefits from AM. 70% stays traditional. This is the RIGHT ratio.**
