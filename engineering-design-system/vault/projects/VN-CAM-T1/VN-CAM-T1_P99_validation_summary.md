## 6. LESSONS LEARNED (D-M-I-R)

### 6.1 Diagnosis (What Worked)

**Integration Success:**
- ODI identified exact customer pain points (T1-01: 16.0)
- Systems thinking revealed feedback loops (3 virtuous, 1 balancing)
- P&B structured requirements systematically (96.5% quantified)
- No framework conflicts - they reinforced each other

**Key Insight:**
> "ODI tells us WHAT customers want (outcomes), Systems Thinking tells us HOW the system works (feedback loops), and Pahl & Beitz tells us WHAT to build (requirements). Together, they create complete understanding."

**Innovation Success Factors:**
- EXTREME opportunity identified (T1-01: 16.0) → High success probability
- Leverage points targeted (L6, L9, L10) → High-impact interventions
- VDI 2225 weighted by ODI → Customer-driven concept selection

---

### 6.2 Modeling (What Could Be Improved)

**Data Gaps:**
- ODI importance/satisfaction scores are hypothesized (need customer interviews)
- Vietnamese shooter AI dataset does not yet exist (need to build R9206)
- LOMAH integration details not fully defined (need hardware interface spec)

**Process Improvements:**
- Create ODI survey automation tool (reduce interview time)
- Develop CLD template library (accelerate systems analysis)
- Build requirement traceability database (manage 143 requirements)

**Technical Risks:**
- Thermal management required operating temp revision (-10°C to +50°C, not +55°C)
- Assembly time (120 min) exceeds target (30 min) - needs process optimization
- Die-cast tooling ($50K, 12 weeks) is long lead time - use CNC for pilot
