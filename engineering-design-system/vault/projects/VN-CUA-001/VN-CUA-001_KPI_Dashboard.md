# VN-CUA-001 KPI DASHBOARD

## Bảng điều khiển KPI - Hệ thống Chống Drone

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This dashboard monitors the **key feedback loops** and **leverage points** identified in the VN-CUA-001 Systems Analysis. KPIs are organized by:

1. **Loop Health Indicators** - Are feedback loops activating as planned?
2. **Leading Indicators** - Early warning signals
3. **Lagging Indicators** - Outcome confirmation
4. **Intervention Triggers** - When to take action

**Primary Focus:** R3 (Evidence Preservation) and R4 (Training Excellence) as key differentiators.
