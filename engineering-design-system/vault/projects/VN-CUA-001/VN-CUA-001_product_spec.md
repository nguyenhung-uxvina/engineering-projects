## 2. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

### 2.1 Core Principles

#### Principle 1: FORENSIC CAPTURE (Bắt giữ nguyên vẹn)
- Net capture preserves drone intact for investigation
- Parachute ensures soft landing, minimal damage
- Critical advantage over RF jammers (which crash drones)

#### Principle 2: UNIVERSAL EFFECTIVENESS (Hiệu quả phổ quát)
- Works against ALL drone types: commercial, FPV, autonomous, military
- No dependence on RF/GPS vulnerabilities
- Kinetic defeat cannot be jammed or spoofed

#### Principle 3: COST-EFFECTIVE SIMPLICITY (Đơn giản hiệu quả)
- 80% of SkyWall capability at 20% of cost
- Simplified targeting (manual tracking acceptable)
- Standard components, local manufacturing

#### Principle 4: VIETNAMESE PRODUCTION (Sản xuất Việt Nam)
- ≥70% local content by value
- Standard manufacturing processes (CNC, injection molding)
- Reduce strategic dependency on imports

## 6. FUNCTION STRUCTURE (From SkyWall RE)

```
VDC-100 FUNCTION STRUCTURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

OVERALL: Capture airborne drone using net projectile with manual targeting

F1: ACQUIRE TARGET
├── F1.1: Detect target visually ─────────── Operator eyes
├── F1.2: Aim at target ──────────────────── Optical scope
├── F1.3: Measure target range ───────────── Laser rangefinder
└── F1.4: Estimate lead angle ────────────── Reticle markings (manual)

F2: PREPARE FIRE
├── F2.1: Load projectile ────────────────── Breech loading
├── F2.2: Charge gas system ──────────────── HPA from cylinder
├── F2.3: Arm safety ─────────────────────── Mechanical switch
└── F2.4: Confirm ready status ───────────── Indicator

F3: EXECUTE FIRE
├── F3.1: Align with target ──────────────── Operator aiming
├── F3.2: Pull trigger ───────────────────── Mechanical linkage
├── F3.3: Release gas ────────────────────── Solenoid/hammer valve
└── F3.4: Propel projectile ──────────────── Gas expansion

F4: DEPLOY CAPTURE
├── F4.1: Fly to target vicinity ─────────── Ballistic trajectory
├── F4.2: Deploy net ─────────────────────── Timer/barometric trigger
├── F4.3: Spread net ─────────────────────── Corner weights
├── F4.4: Entangle target ────────────────── Net engulfs drone
└── F4.5: Deploy parachute ───────────────── Drogue extraction

F5: RECOVER TARGET
├── F5.1: Slow descent ───────────────────── Parachute drag
└── F5.2: Land safely ────────────────────── Controlled impact

F_AUX: AUXILIARY
├── F_AUX.1: Store gas energy ────────────── HPA cylinder
├── F_AUX.2: Regulate pressure ───────────── Regulator
├── F_AUX.3: Power scope ─────────────────── Battery
├── F_AUX.4: Indicate status ─────────────── LED/display
└── F_AUX.5: Protect system ──────────────── Safety mechanisms

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TOTAL: 5 Main Functions + 1 Auxiliary = 6 Function Groups, 23 Subfunctions
```
