## STEP 6: OPPORTUNITY SCORE CALCULATION

### 6.1 Methodology

**Opportunity Algorithm:**
```
Opportunity = Importance + MAX(Importance - Satisfaction, 0)
```

**Conversion (5-point to 10-point):**
```
Score_10 = (% responding 4 or 5) × 10
```

**Interpretation:**
- **>15:** EXTREME opportunity (rare, highest value)
- **12-15:** HIGH opportunity (strong priority)
- **10-12:** MODERATE opportunity (monitor)
- **<10:** LOW opportunity (potentially overserved)

### 7.1 Segmentation Analysis

#### SEGMENT A: "Rapid Responders" (Nhanh chóng) - 40%

**Typical Use Case:**

- Fast power-on (instant-on targeting)
- Quick reload (<10 sec)
- Always-ready carry mode
- Multiple projectile capacity

**Profile:** Prioritize accuracy and first-shot hit probability.

**Key Underserved Outcomes:**

**Typical Use Case:**

**Product Implications:**
- Advanced targeting optics
- Ballistic computer (lead calculation)

- Practice/training mode for proficiency

### 7.3 Segment Decision: Design for Largest Segment

**PRIMARY TARGET:** Rapid Responders (40%)

**Rationale:**
1. Largest segment = most units sold
2. Speed outcomes align with Vietnamese operational reality (drones appear suddenly)
3. Design for speed benefits all segments (faster is always better)
4. Can add Precision Seeker features as optional upgrade

- Operator stress affects aim

**Solution Ideas:**

| 4 | Longer barrel (higher velocity, flatter trajectory) | HIGH | +$30 | MEDIUM |
| 5 | Gyro-stabilized optic | LOW | +$2,000 | HIGH |
| 6 | Training simulator mode | MEDIUM | +$100 | MEDIUM |
| 7 | Trigger with half-pull pre-staging | HIGH | +$20 | MEDIUM |
| 8 | ★ Combined: LRF + ballistic reticle + longer barrel | HIGH | +$280 | **VERY HIGH** |

**SELECTED SOLUTION:** #8 (Combined approach)
- LRF gives accurate range
- Ballistic reticle shows correct holdover
- Longer barrel = flatter trajectory = easier aim
- Total cost impact: +$280 (within budget)
