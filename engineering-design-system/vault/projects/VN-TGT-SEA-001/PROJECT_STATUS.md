## Product Description
Anchored stationary sea target for anti-ship missile acceptance testing — the ONLY affordable product with frigate-class RCS (>1,000 m²) and storm-survivable deployment:
- **Layer 0:** Storm-rated mooring (SS 5-6, Danforth/Bruce + 12-16mm chain/rode, pre-deployable)
- **Layer 1:** HDPE hull (8.0m diameter) with closed-cell marine foam fill
- **Layer 2:** Steel structural frame (reinforced mooring attachment, self-draining deck)
- **Layer 2.5:** 8× galvanized steel masts (60mm×4mm, ~3m height, deck sockets)
- **Layer 3:** 8× hybrid corner reflectors (0.8m, CNC faces + AM frames, 1,000-1,200 m², at 3-4m AGL)
