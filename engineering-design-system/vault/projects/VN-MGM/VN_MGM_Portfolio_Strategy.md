# VN-MGM PRODUCT PORTFOLIO STRATEGY
## Naval/Defense Machine Gun Mount Product Family
## Danh mục Sản phẩm Giá Súng máy Hải quân/Quốc phòng

---

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

## Current State
The VN-MGM project currently focuses on a single product family:
- **VN-MGM-001**: 12.7mm Naval Gun Mount System (A/B/C)

## Proposed Portfolio Expansion
Expand to a **3-tier, multi-caliber product family** addressing:
- Multiple weapon calibers (7.62mm to 14.5mm)
- Multiple platforms (naval, vehicle, fixed)
- Multiple market segments (military, coast guard, militia)
